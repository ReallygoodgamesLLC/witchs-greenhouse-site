Witch's Greenhouse Support

Help with the game, purchases and your saved progress.

Contact us

Email reallygoodgamesllc@gmail.com and include your device (for example iPhone 15 or iPad Air) and what happened. We usually reply within two business days.

Common questions

How do I keep my progress if I get a new phone?

Sign in to Game Center on your device (Settings, then Game Center). The game saves your progress to iCloud through Game Center and brings it back when you install it on another iPhone or iPad with the same Apple Account.

I bought gems but didn't get them.

Close the game completely and open it again while connected to the internet. If the gems still haven't arrived, email us with the date of the purchase and the receipt from Apple, and we'll sort it out.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple. You can request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

I ran out of moves. What can I do?

Use 5 gems for extra moves, or try the level again. If you fail a level 5 times, the extra moves grow: +10, then +15 and so on, up to +40.

How do I unlock new greenhouses?

Buy repairs, plants, furniture and pets in the Store. Every purchase gives stars, and stars unlock the next greenhouse. Tap the star at the top left to see how close you are.

Where are the leaderboards?

Tap the gold trophy on the greenhouse screen. You need to be signed in to Game Center for your scores to appear.

The music stopped.

Tap the screen once. If it's still quiet, check that Music is on in the game's Settings (the gear at the top right) and that your device isn't on silent.
