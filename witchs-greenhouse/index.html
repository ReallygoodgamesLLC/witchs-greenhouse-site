Swap sunflowers, bluebells and berries into rows of three. Match four or more to brew Fizzing Flasks, Boom Brews and Love Potions, then set them off for big, bubbly chain reactions.

Restore and decorate

Every win earns coins to spend on repairs, plants, cozy furniture and decorations. Drag everything wherever you like and watch the witch tend her garden.

Greenhouses of the world
