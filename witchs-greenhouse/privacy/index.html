Witch's Greenhouse Privacy Policy

Last updated October 26, 2026

Witch's Greenhouse is a match-3 and decorating game for iPhone and iPad. This page explains what information the game uses and why.

What stays on your device

Your progress, coins, gems, plants, pets, settings and the name you give your witch are saved only on your device. We don't have an account system and we don't receive this information. Deleting the app deletes this saved progress.

Purchases

If you buy a gem pack, the payment is handled by Apple. We never see your name, email address or payment details. We use RevenueCat, a purchase service, to confirm with Apple that a purchase is real so the gems can be added. RevenueCat receives a record of the purchase (which pack, the date and an anonymous ID created for your device). This record is not linked to your identity and is used only to make purchases work. You can read RevenueCat's privacy policy.

What we don't do

  • No ads and no advertising trackers.
  • No analytics or usage tracking.
  • No location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone access.
  • We don't sell or share your information with anyone.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages and doesn't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
